Talk at the Project Jupyter Mini Summit, part of Open Source Summit + Embedded Linux Conference Europe 2026 in Prague.

How the Jupyter Security Council handles a growing volume of vulnerability reports across Jupyter's many repositories, including efforts to reduce the triage backlog and launch long-term supply chain and documentation initiatives. The talk also looks at the benefits and drawbacks of AI for finding vulnerabilities and triaging reports, and invites the community to get involved.
